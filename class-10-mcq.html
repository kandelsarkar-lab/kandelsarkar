COMMON QUESTIONS

Class 10 MCQ NEB FAQ

What is Class 10 MCQ NEB?

Class 10 MCQ NEB is free chapter-wise multiple-choice practice for the Nepal Education Board SEE examination. It covers Science, Mathematics and Computer Science for Grade 10.

How many Class 10 MCQs are available?

The site has more than 4,400 MCQs: 1,900+ Science questions across 19 units, 1,400+ Mathematics questions across 14 chapters and 1,100+ Computer Science questions across 11 units.

Does the site save my test progress?

Yes. Every completed practice test and mock exam is saved in your browser on this device. You can see scores, study time, accuracy, weak topics and full test history without creating an account.

How does weak topic identification work?

The site groups your answers by Science unit, Math chapter or Computer unit. Any topic where your accuracy falls below 65% after at least two questions is shown in the weak topic radar with a direct practice link.

Is there a full SEE mock exam?

Yes. The full SEE mock exam has 100 MCQs from Science, Mathematics and Computer Science, a fixed 3-hour countdown, auto-submit, a question palette, mark-for-review and detailed result analysis.

Is my progress private?

Yes. Progress is stored locally on your device using browser storage and a small first-party progress cookie. No account, name or personal data is sent to a server.
